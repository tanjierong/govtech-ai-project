# Scenario A: ServiceSG enquiry response

## The situation

ServiceSG answers a large volume of resident enquiries across contact centres, front desks and helpdesks. Too many replies miss the service level target, first replies to email and web enquiries are slow, and many residents have to contact the agency a second time.

## What the team found

- Officers spend more time finding the right policy page than writing the reply.
- Two officers answering the same question often give different answers.
- Many enquiries include an image or attachment that current tools cannot read.
- Policy changes often, and the source of truth sits with several teams.

## Constraints

- Some enquiries involve personal data, complaints or vulnerable residents.
- Every reply goes out under a government name.
- The owners will not hire their way out of rising volume.
- A wrong answer costs more than a slow one.

## Data rule

Make up every enquiry, resident and policy page you use. Never paste a real enquiry or real resident details.
