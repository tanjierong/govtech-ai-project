# Scenario C: MOE lower secondary biology curriculum

## The situation

MOE is rolling out a revised biology curriculum for lower secondary combined science to every secondary school, with a short deadline for schools to be ready. Many schools do not feel ready yet. HQ receives many enquiries from biology teachers, and most repeat a question already answered. Building one aligned biology assessment takes a teacher far longer than the target.

## What the team found

- Teachers understood the direction but did not have time to turn it into lessons and assessments.
- Some HQ replies were correct for the wrong science strand.
- Heads of department could not tell which teachers were falling behind until it was too late.

## Constraints

- Student data and assessment integrity must be protected.
- Anything that produces teaching or assessment content needs a person accountable for what goes in front of students.
- Teachers will trust a new tool only if their first experience is good.

## Data rule

Make up every teacher, student, enquiry and assessment you use. Never paste real student records or real assessment items.
