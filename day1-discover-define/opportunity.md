# Opportunity: Pre-submission: The stage before an applicant is about to submit their application

**Scenario:** B · ICA passport applications

All quotes and metrics are synthetic and labelled as made up.

## Problem Framework

### 1. Goal

Fewer returns and shorter end-to-end application time

### 2. Problem

17% of submissions are returned for errors or missing documents, adding ~6 days each. Applicants don’t know what a complete application looks like for their situation, and only 38% read the guidance before applying — so generic guidance isn’t working.

### 3. Context

520,000 applications and renewals a year go through an online channel.

### 4. Constraints

Final decisions stay with ICA officers. The vendor withdraws in 10 weeks and the date won’t move.

### 5. Success

<5% returns rate by officers

### 6. Explore

Can a vetter correctly identify the application's issues and successfully influence applicants to actually fix what it flags?

## Evidence (synthetic)

### User quotes

- “I renewed mine fine, but for my daughter I had no idea I needed both parents to sign — it came back after a week.”
- “I only found out the photo was too old after they returned it. Why couldn’t it tell me before I submitted?”
- “The guidance page is long and none of it felt like it was about my case.”

### Metrics

- agreement rate between vetter and officer manual checks
- changes by applicants post-vetting
- submissions returned for errors or missing document
- median time taken for end-to-end application

## Hypothesis

By providing an automated vetter to passport applicants before they submit, I can reduce the return rate of applications, reducing the amount of time overall

- Risk type: Value (build a landing page first)
- Kill switch: Applicants will follow instructions from an automated vetter
- Kill-switch signal: Applicants make no change after seeing the suggestions
- Success signal: Delta in application quality, measured by return rate, from 17% to 10%
